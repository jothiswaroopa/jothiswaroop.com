Questions

Can AI-made ads run on Meta, TikTok and YouTube?

Yes. Each platform has its own disclosure rules for AI-generated content, and I label every ad accordingly. I never use real people's likeness or fake reviews.

Who owns the ads?

You do. Final files are yours to run anywhere, with no monthly usage fees.

What do you need from me?

Product photos or a shipped sample, a 20-minute call, and a yes or no on scripts within a day.

Will it look like AI?

That is the bar I work to. Product labels, faces and locations are locked as references, and any take that would not pass in a real edit is re-made before you see it.

Can you keep the same creator across all our ads?

Yes. A consistent brand creator across testimonials, podcasts and demos is one of the things AI does better than casting.
