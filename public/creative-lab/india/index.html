How I test ads

Same ad. Five different openings. Your viewers pick the winner.

People decide in the first three seconds whether to keep watching. So I make one ad and give it five different openings. All five run at the same time, and the opening that keeps the most people watching becomes the start of your next ads. Tap one to watch it.

Why it works: only the first few seconds change, so the test costs almost nothing extra. And you find out exactly which opening makes people stop scrolling. The winner becomes the opening of your next batch.

Yes. Each platform has its own label for AI-generated content, and I flag every ad so you can apply it. I never use a real person's likeness and never write fake reviews.
