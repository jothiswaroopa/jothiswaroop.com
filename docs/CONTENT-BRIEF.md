# Content Brief — jothiswaroop.com

## A. Videos (only #1 is essential for launch)

### 1. Hero video — the one that matters most
Where: right side of the hero, replacing the portrait. Autoplays muted with captions; tap for sound.
Length: 60–90 s. Format: vertical 9:16 on phone; also a horizontal 16:9 take if possible.
Setup: phone at eye level on a stand · face the window (light on your face, never behind) · plain quiet background · wired-earphone mic or phone mic within 1 m · first-meeting clothes · look at the LENS.
Script skeleton (your words, not read):
 0–5s   Name + one fact. "I'm Jothi. Last year one client got 3,222 leads from a system I built."
 5–20s  Who it's for. "If you run a brand and you've paid an agency, you probably got a monthly PDF and no customers."
 20–45s What you do, plainly. "I run your ads, build the creatives, set up the automation that follows up with every lead — one person, no account managers. You get a weekly number, not a report."
 45–65s Proof + how you get clients. "Every client I have was introduced by a client I have. I've never sent a cold pitch."
 65–80s Ask. "If that sounds like what you want, apply below. Five questions. I read every one myself."
Do: 3 full takes, send all. Don't: music, montages, stock footage, on-screen effects, script voice.
